# KPI Submission — User Guide

**Gamification Division FY2026 Dashboard**

---

## What is KPI Submission?

KPI Submission is the module where each team member reports their actual performance results every quarter. Your actuals are compared against pre-set level thresholds (L1–L5) to determine your performance score.

---

## Step-by-Step: How to Submit Your KPI

### Step 1 — Log In

1. Open the dashboard in your browser.
2. Enter your **username** (e.g. `junialdi`, `auliya`, `sandi`) and **access code**.
3. Click **Login**.

> You will land on the main dashboard. Your name appears in the top-right corner.

---

### Step 2 — Go to KPI Submission

1. In the left sidebar, click **KPI Submission** (icon: 📝).
2. Your name is automatically selected (non-admin users cannot switch person).
3. Select the **Quarter** you are reporting for: **Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4**.

---

### Step 3 — Fill in Your KPI Actuals

Each KPI card shows:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **KPI Name** | The metric you are reporting |
| **Perspective** | Financial / Customer / Internal / Learning |
| **Weight** | How much this KPI counts toward your overall score |
| **Unit (UoM)** | The unit of measurement (%, IDR, count, etc.) |
| **L1–L5 buttons** | Level thresholds — tap to quick-fill a mid-point value |
| **Actual input** | Enter your real achieved number here |

**To enter your actual:**

- **Option A — Tap a Level button**: Click L1, L2, L3, L4, or L5 to auto-fill the midpoint value for that level.
- **Option B — Type directly**: Click the input field and type your exact number.

> The card updates the level badge (L1–L5) and progress bar in real time as you type.

---

### Step 4 — Add Evidence (Optional but Recommended)

Click **Evidence & Attachments** to expand the evidence section:

1. **URL**: Paste a Google Drive, Notion, or Confluence link that supports your actual.
2. **File Upload**: Upload PDFs, images, Excel files, or CSVs as proof.
3. **Note**: Add a written explanation or context.

> Evidence strengthens your submission and helps the reviewer approve faster.

---

### Step 5 — Add a Custom KPI (if needed)

If you have a KPI that is not in your standard set:

1. Click the **+ Add Custom KPI** tile (dashed border at the end of the card grid).
2. Fill in:
   - **KPI Name** — what you are measuring
   - **Perspective** — which BSC perspective it belongs to
   - **Weight** — how much it contributes (%)
   - **Unit** — the unit of measurement
   - **L1–L5 thresholds** — the values for each performance level (L1 = minimum, L5 = excellence). L2 and L4 will auto-fill if left empty.
3. Click **Add KPI**. The new card appears and you can fill in your actual immediately.

---

### Step 6 — Review Your Score Summary

Below the KPI cards, the **Score Summary** panel shows:

- **Weighted Score / 5** — your overall weighted performance score
- **Level breakdown** — how many KPIs fall at each level (L1–L5)
- **Total KPIs and weight**

Use this to check your submission before sending.

---

### Step 7 — Save or Submit

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Save as Draft** | Saves your progress — you can return and edit later |
| **Submit for Approval →** | Locks the submission and sends it to your reviewer |

> Once submitted, status changes to **Pending**. You cannot edit until the reviewer requests a revision.

---

## Submission Status Flow

```
Draft → Pending → Approved
                ↘ Revision → (you edit) → Pending → Approved
```

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Draft | Saved but not submitted |
| Pending | Submitted, awaiting reviewer |
| Revision | Reviewer requested changes — check the comment banner |
| Approved | Finalized |
| Rejected | Not accepted — contact your atasan |

---

## If a Revision is Requested

1. An orange banner appears at the top of your submission form with the reviewer's comment.
2. Update your actuals or evidence based on the feedback.
3. Click **Submit for Approval →** again to re-submit.

---

## FAQ

**Q: I don't see any KPI cards.**
A: Your KPI data may not be configured yet. Contact admin (Junialdi or Shieny) to set up your PERSON_KPIS.

**Q: Can I change my submission after submitting?**
A: Only if the reviewer sends it back as "Revision". Once Approved it is final.

**Q: What is the deadline for Q1 submission?**
A: Q1 deadline is end of March (31 March 2026). Submit before the deadline to allow time for review.

**Q: My actual is between two level thresholds — what level will I get?**
A: The system assigns the highest level whose threshold your actual meets or exceeds. E.g., if L3 = 80% and L4 = 90%, and your actual is 85%, you are L3.
